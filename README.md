# UNRE HR

University HR for the **Papua New Guinea University of Natural Resources and Environment**. Two portals share the same records.

## Two interfaces

**HR office** (`hr@unre.ac.pg` / `unre2026`)

- Workforce dashboard, employees, departments
- Approve or reject leave and loans filed by staff
- Job postings and payroll register (NASFUND 6%, PAYE, loan recoveries)

**Staff self-service** (any employee in the sign-in list / `unre2026`)

- Request leave and staff loans
- See status of those requests
- Monthly payslip with PNG-style deductions
- Apply for internal jobs
- View own profile
- HR can sign an accepted internal applicant's contract with an agreed annual salary; the employee's role, appointment, department, and payslip update after signing.

Approved loans deduct from the monthly payslip. Policy: one outstanding loan, maximum 40% of annual salary.

## Open the app

Open `index.html` in a browser, or use a local static server. Data stays in this browser (`localStorage`).
